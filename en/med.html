Where copper scrap comes from

Over 27 years we've accepted copper from all kinds of sources. Private individuals bring old plumbing fixtures (taps, mixers, pipes) after renovation, copper wire from rewiring jobs, radiators from old cars and boilers. Factories and businesses bring production offcuts, copper shavings from machining, and decommissioned electrical equipment. Electricians bring cable offcuts left over from wiring installations.

Car workshops bring copper-brass radiators, starter and alternator windings. Construction companies bring copper earthing bars and wiring remnants. All accepted with no minimum — from 1 kg.

What affects the copper price per kilogram

  • Grade and purity. Bright copper (Millberry) — maximum price. Mix, oxidised scrap, items with solder — slightly less.
  • Insulation. PVC or rubber-insulated cable is priced by copper content %, determined by our analyser.
  • Contamination. Copper mixed with steel, plastic or aluminium is assessed with a deduction for impurities.
  • Volume. Large batches from 500 kg — negotiated individually, price may be higher.
  • Condition. Burnt wires or fire-damaged copper are worth less — oxidation reduces purity.
