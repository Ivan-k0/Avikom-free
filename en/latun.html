How to tell brass from copper and bronze?

Brass is yellow-gold, copper is pink-red, bronze is reddish-brown. Brass is harder than copper and machines well. If you're unsure — don't guess. Bring it in, and our specialists will identify the metal in 30 seconds with a free spectral analysis and quote an exact price.
