How much is scrap metal worth in Odessa today

Scrap metal prices in Odessa change almost daily — they depend directly on global metal quotations, the hryvnia exchange rate, and demand from processing plants. What determines the price of scrap metal

  • Metal type and grade. Pure bright copper costs many times more than mixed ferrous scrap.
  • Global LME quotations. London Metal Exchange prices directly influence purchasing prices in Ukraine.
  • Dollar exchange rate. Metal is traded in foreign currency, so exchange rate fluctuations reflect in hryvnia prices.
  • Batch volume. When selling from 1 tonne, many collection points including Avto-Vikom offer better terms and free pickup.

Why prices differ between collection points

Many small buyers add their own margin — 10-25 UAH per kilogram, especially for non-ferrous metals. Avto-Vikom works directly with metallurgical plants without intermediaries, so we can pay clients more than the average market price in Odessa.
